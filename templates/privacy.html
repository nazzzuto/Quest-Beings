Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Quest-Beings is a personal learning project. This page explains what data it uses.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. If you sign up with email, we store your name, email and a hashed password. We also store your quiz scores and streaks.

How we use it

Only to log you in, show your name and avatar, and display the leaderboard. We do not sell or share your data with anyone, and we do not send marketing emails.

Deleting your data

To have your account and data deleted, email the site owner at nazathbarbhuiya42@gmail.com.
